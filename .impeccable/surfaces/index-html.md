# Surface brief: cinematic story page (/)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: owners and directors of SMBs in Uzbekistan. Action: «Узнать стоимость» → cloudplus.uz.
Proof: each chapter shows a concrete automated process in Cloudplus's own UI over real footage of that business.
Constraints: free Pexels footage only; static GitHub Pages; Russian; the logo floats above the city (v8).
Structure pinned by the client: the same story as the promo video (production → warehouse & delivery → coffee
shop → supermarket → one program → floating logo), so no structure roll; the visual world extends cloudplus.uz.

## Direction contract

seed: none (structure and world pinned by the client brief and the existing brand)

THESIS: The page is a film you scroll. Real footage of a business at work, and the Cloudplus screen appears
exactly where that work happens. Refuses the category default: SaaS hero with a dashboard screenshot, feature
grid, three cards, logo wall.

OWN-WORLD: Full-bleed documentary footage graded cool in the shadows, warm in the practical lights; the
product UI as cloudplus.uz draws it (#0B0C10 panels, #14161D cards, Geist, status chips); brand blue #3FA9F5
only on live UI state and the mark; titles Onest 600 at cinema scale in white over a bottom scrim; the site's
vertical light beam is the single recurring signature (hero, chapter seams, finale).

STORY: Night city, the mark hovers over it → «Программа под ваш бизнес» → Производство → Склад и доставка →
Кофейня (касса, деньги, сырьё → автозаказ, поставщики) → Магазин → «Вся компания в одной программе» →
the mark returns over the city, «Узнать стоимость».

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed night-city footage; the 3D cloud mark hovering above the skyline on a vertical
beam; «Программа под ваш бизнес» / «Работает через 14 дней»; a scroll cue. No nav bar beyond the wordmark
and one CTA.

SIGNATURE: Scroll scrubs each chapter's footage frame by frame while its UI assembles in step with the action
(a coffee is paid, the receipt line lands; a pallet leaves, stock ticks down).

RISK: Footage weight and mobile performance; generic stock reading as stock. Mitigate: few, carefully chosen
clips, small intra-frame encodes, lazy per chapter, a poster frame per chapter.
