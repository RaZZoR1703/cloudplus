# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners and directors of small and medium businesses in Uzbekistan whose accounting still lives in spreadsheets and chats (sales, stock, money, production) and who are weighing a program built for their own company. They watch the site to understand, concretely, what such a program would take over in a business like theirs.

## Product Purpose

A cinematic promo site for Cloudplus: it shows, business by business, which processes Cloudplus automates, and sends the visitor to cloudplus.uz to request a price. Success: the visitor recognises their own business in one chapter and clicks «Узнать стоимость».

## Positioning

Cloudplus builds a custom CRM/ERP for the client's own processes (not a boxed product): a working version on the client's data in 14 days, the price fixed in the contract, the source code stays with the client. Integrates both ways with 1С, CRM, cash registers and websites. IT Park resident, 150+ clients, 60+ people in the team (all from cloudplus.uz).

## Operating Context

Watched on desktop and phones, in Russian. Businesses shown: production, warehouse and delivery (logistics), coffee shop, supermarket/retail. For a coffee shop the client named: own POS/cash register, income and expense tracking, raw-material stock (coffee packs), supplier information; other businesses get the same "what is automated here" treatment.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site on GitHub Pages (Vite + three.js already in the repo), deployed by GitHub Actions; `/city/` keeps the earlier 3D city, `/v8/` the original presentation.
- Footage: free Pexels videos only (Pexels License: commercial use, no attribution). No paid generation services.
- Main CTA: «Узнать стоимость» → https://cloudplus.uz/.
- Russian only.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Cloudplus. Mark: the cloud-with-swoosh-and-star logo (vector in `video/assets/mark-*.svg`, from cloudplus.uz). The logo must float above the ground/city, as in the v8 presentation.
- Site copy to reuse verbatim: «Программа под ваш бизнес. Работает через 14 дней», «Вся компания в одной программе», «Остатки сходятся с продажами без пересчета вручную», «Склад, продажи и деньги в одной системе».
- Brand palette and fonts from cloudplus.uz: ink #0A1B33, blue #3FA9F5 → #0B3A8C, product UI on #0B0C10 / #14161D; Geist and Onest.

## Evidence on Hand

- Copy, stats and UI examples captured from cloudplus.uz in `video/capture/` (screens of Финансы, CRM, Склад, Производство, Касса).
- Case: APP1 moved from spreadsheets to one system (site copy). No other testimonials, client logos for use, or prices may be invented; UI numbers are illustrative.

## Product Principles

1. Show the process, not the slogan: every chapter proves one concrete thing the program does.
2. The visitor's own business first: each chapter must be recognisable at a glance.
3. Real over drawn: real footage of real places; the product UI in Cloudplus's own style.
4. One clear next step: «Узнать стоимость» on cloudplus.uz.
